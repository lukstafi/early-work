# Coq as a Tutor in Formal Reasoning (2008)

Slides for a seminar talk given in early February 2008 at a students-and-faculty offsite of the Institute of Computer Science, University of Wrocław.

- `PapugTutor.pdf` (source `PapugTutor.odp`): the talk as given. It starts from the mistakes students make in proofs, presents "naive type theory" as a better basis for teaching than sets everywhere, and shows how an assistant inside a prover can propose the next step.
- `IntProv.odp`: an earlier and longer version, "Interactive Proving with Coq: proving with mouse and some examples".

The talk reports on other people's work, credited on its last slide: "Proof by Pointing" by Bertot, Kahn and Théry (1994); "Extracting Text from Proof" by Coscoy, Kahn and Théry; the Coq 8.1 reference manual; and "Papuq: a Coq assistant" by Sakowicz and Chrząszcz (2007). Screenshots and figures in the slides are from those sources.

The Coq library and exercises shown in the talk are not included. They are not the speaker's: his homepage of the time listed them as "exemplary exercises from the Warsaw University Institute of Informatics".
